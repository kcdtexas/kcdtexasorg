# KCD Texas Website

The official website of KCD Texas, a CNCF-sanctioned Kubernetes Community Day in Texas. Organizers build and run it for attendees, speakers and sponsors.

## Language

### Event

**KCD Texas**:
The yearly, community-run Kubernetes Community Day for Texas, sanctioned by CNCF.
_Avoid_: KCD Austin (the name used on the 2026 CNCF issue), KCDTX

**Cloud Native Texas**:
The Texas cloud native community (Austin, Dallas and Houston Chapters) that runs monthly meetups. KCD Texas is its annual conference. The Website does not cover it.
_Avoid_: Using it as a synonym for KCD Texas

**Edition**:
One year's KCD Texas event, with its own date, city, venue, program and sponsors (for example, the 2027 Edition). The city can change from Edition to Edition. Each Edition has a status: planned, postponed or canceled.
_Avoid_: Event year, iteration, instance, "the event" when the year matters

**Current Edition**:
The one Edition the Website's homepage presents.

**Past Edition**:
An Edition that has already taken place. The Website keeps it as a permanent record. A canceled Edition (such as 2023) is not a Past Edition.
_Avoid_: Old event

**Tier**:
CNCF's classification of a KCD, which sets its registration cap and the support it gets. "Tier 2" and "Independent" are the same tier (CNCF renamed it for 2027).

### Phases

**Phase**:
A stage in the Current Edition's lifecycle that sets whom the Website serves first. The Phases run in order: CFP Phase, Countdown, Event Day, Recap.

**CFP Phase**:
From the Edition's announcement until the CFP closes. Sponsors come first, then prospective Speakers.

**Countdown**:
From the CFP closing until the event starts. Attendees come first. The Schedule appears during this Phase, not at its start.
_Avoid_: Agenda Live (the Schedule is published up to two weeks after the Phase begins)

**Event Day**:
The day or days of the event itself. Attendees on site come first.

**Recap**:
From the end of the event until the next Edition becomes the Current Edition. The next Edition's sponsors come first, then past attendees.

### Program

**CFP**:
An Edition's call for proposals, run on Sessionize by the Session Committee.
_Avoid_: Call for Speakers, call for papers, C4P

**Session**:
One item in an Edition's program (a keynote, talk, workshop or panel), given by one or more Speakers.
_Avoid_: Talk (when you mean any kind of Session), slot

**End-User Story**:
A Session in which an End User shares how their own organization runs cloud native in production. The Schedule labels these Sessions publicly.
_Avoid_: Case study (vendor case studies are marketing), customer story

**Speaker**:
A person presenting in one Edition's program. The same person speaking in two Editions counts as two Speakers.
_Avoid_: Presenter, talent

**Schedule**:
An Edition's Sessions placed into time slots and rooms. It is published during Countdown.
_Avoid_: Agenda (CNCF's word for the same thing), grid, timetable

**My Schedule**:
An attendee's personal selection of Sessions, kept only in their own browser.
_Avoid_: Favorites, personal agenda, bookmarks

### Audience

**Registration**:
A ticket obtained on the Event Page. The Tier's cap counts Registrations, including those of Speakers and Sponsors.
_Avoid_: Signup, booking

**Attendee**:
A person who checked in at an Edition. Attendance figures count Attendees, never Registrations.
_Avoid_: Registrant (for someone who checked in), participant

**Returning Attendee**:
An Attendee who also attended an earlier Edition. Everyone else is a First-Time Attendee.
_Avoid_: Alumni, repeat visitor

**End User**:
An organization that runs cloud native technology for its own business rather than selling it, and the people who work there.
_Avoid_: Customer, enterprise, practitioner (when you mean the organization type)

### Sponsorship

**Sponsor**:
An organization that supports one or more Editions through Sponsorships. A Sponsor persists across Editions. A company that is acquired and later sponsors under its new owner's name counts as a new Sponsor.
_Avoid_: Partner (when money changes hands), vendor

**Sponsorship**:
A Sponsor's support for one Edition: its Sponsor Tier, any Add-ons, and the logo it used that year. A Sponsorship is paid in cash or in kind (goods or services).
_Avoid_: Deal, package, contract

**Sponsor Tier**:
A named level of Sponsorship with a fixed price and benefits, such as Platinum, Gold or Silver.
_Avoid_: Package, level

**Add-on**:
A paid sponsorship of one specific item, such as the lanyards or the happy hour, that forms part of a Sponsorship on top of or instead of a Sponsor Tier.
_Avoid_: Extra, upsell

**Partner**:
A community or media organization that promotes one or more Editions and does not pay. Like a Sponsor, it persists across Editions and is listed for each Edition with a partner type (community or media).
_Avoid_: Sponsor (when no money changes hands)

### Channels

**Website**:
The permanent KCD Texas site on the Organizers' own domain, covering every Edition.
_Avoid_: Landing page, microsite

**Event Page**:
An Edition's listing on community.cncf.io, CNCF's registration platform. Registration and ticket checkout happen only there.
_Avoid_: Bevy page, CNCF site, registration site

**Chapter**:
A group on community.cncf.io that people join to hear about its events. KCD Texas has its own Chapter. The Austin, Dallas and Houston Chapters belong to Cloud Native Texas. Joining the KCD Texas Chapter is how people "get notified".
_Avoid_: Mailing list, newsletter, subscribers

**Short Link**:
A short, memorable kcdtexas.org address that forwards somewhere else. Short Links go on slides, signage and QR codes (for example /cfp or /sponsors).
_Avoid_: Alias, vanity URL, redirect

**Share Card**:
A generated social image announcing that a Sponsor sponsors, or a Speaker speaks at, an Edition.
_Avoid_: Badge (a badge is the physical attendee badge), banner

### People

**Organizer**:
A volunteer on the KCD Texas organizing team, publicly listed with their employer. An Organizer persists across Editions and holds a role in each Edition they help run, such as a Committee seat or Moderator. "Co-Organizer" is a role title within that team, not a separate kind of person.
_Avoid_: Staff, admin, host

**Volunteer**:
A person who helps run Event Day without being an Organizer. Volunteers are credited for each Edition, with their consent.
_Avoid_: Staff, helper, crew

**Committee**:
A functional group of Organizers that owns one area of the event: Finance, Session, Marketing, Venue, Sponsor, Core Event or Volunteer.
_Avoid_: Team, workgroup, squad

**Moderator**:
An Organizer who leads a Committee.
_Avoid_: Lead, chair, owner

**Maintainer**:
An Organizer responsible for the Website's code, who approves code changes. Any Organizer can approve content changes.
_Avoid_: Webmaster, admin
