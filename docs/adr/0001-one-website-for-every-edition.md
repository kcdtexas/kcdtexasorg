# One permanent website for every Edition

KCD Texas gets one lasting website on its own domain. It presents the Current Edition and keeps every Past Edition as a permanent record, instead of a new site each year or a page inside a wider community site. Organizing teams change every year, and a single codebase that each team refills is the setup most likely to survive those handovers. Years of history are also what sponsors look for.

## Considered Options

- **A new site per Edition** (for example, year subdomains). The work is thrown away every year, and past years disappear or go stale. In 2026, kcdtexas.org forwarded to the CNCF event page instead of hosting its own content.
- **One site for all of Cloud Native Texas** (Chapters, meetups and KCD). It has different owners and dilutes the focus on tickets and sponsors. Cloud Native Texas will get its own site as a separate project.
