# Year-scoped URLs, with Short Links for the Current Edition

Everything that belongs to one Edition lives under its year, for example `/2027/schedule` or `/2027/speakers/jane-doe`. Pages that don't change from year to year live at the root. QR codes, slide decks and YouTube descriptions outlive an Edition, so a URL must never change meaning. Short Links such as `/cfp`, `/schedule` and `/sponsors` point to whatever the Current Edition needs.

## Consequences

- The Short Links that kcdtexas.org served in 2026 are carried over deliberately:
  - General ones follow the Current Edition.
  - Ones tied to 2026 (such as `/present` and `/happy-hour`) stay pinned to 2026.
  - The old catch-all redirect is removed, so unknown addresses show a helpful 404 page.
- All Short Links live in one list that the Marketing Committee owns. None is dropped silently.
